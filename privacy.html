Raise privacy notice

Last updated 13 November 2026. Raise is a student capstone project (Airtribe AI-First Product Management) in a test phase.

What Raise is

Raise helps Indian parents of 14 to 18 year olds prepare for one conversation at a time with their teenager. It gives information for parents. It is not medical, psychological or legal advice. If a child is in danger, call 1098 (Childline) or 14416 (Tele-MANAS).

What we store

  • If you sign in with Google: your name and email address from Google, so you can find your progress again. We do not get your Google password, contacts or files.
  • If you do not sign in: a random ID for this phone. It is not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress: the topics you opened, chapters heard, the day and place you planned to talk, your answers after the talk, family notes and settings.
  • How the app is used: which screens were opened and when, and questions typed into "Ask a doubt". We use this only to improve Raise.

Raise never asks for your child's name, photo or contact details, and it never labels your child.

Who can see it

Your data is stored in Supabase (Mumbai region) and protected so that each account can read only its own progress. Questions typed into "Ask a doubt" are sent to Google's Gemini model to write an answer from the lesson. We do not sell or share your data, and there are no ads.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Me → Account → Delete everything and type DELETE. This removes your progress and usage history from Raise. You can also email the support address shown on the Google sign-in screen.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date above will change.
