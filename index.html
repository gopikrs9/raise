Chapter 3 of 8 · about 2 min to read
What your child may be going through

At this age, friends matter more than almost anything. For many teenagers the phone is where those friends are.

So when you ask them to put it down, it can feel like being asked to leave the room where everyone is talking.

You could say
"I noticed the light from your room was on quite late last night, and I wanted to check how you are holding up with your sleep."

Start from what you have seen, not from a rule. A question opens a door that an order closes.

Start with

Keep in mind
  • Breathe before you begin.
  • If they go quiet, wait.
  • If they say stop, stop. Two minutes counts.
If they ask "why now?"
"Because your health and well-being matter deeply to me, and lack of sleep harms your ability to focus and enjoy your day."

What Raise stores

In plain words. Nothing here is hidden in small print.

Stored for you
  • Your sign-in and first name
  • Your child's age band
  • Topics, plans and what you tell Raise after a talk
  • Your family notes, in your words
Sent to the AI service to write your reflection
  • Age band, topic, and a short summary of your notes
  • Never your name, number or email
Never collected
  • Your child's name, school or photo
  • Your location or contacts
  • Anything from your child's phone

How lessons are checked

  1. 1Facts come only from named sourcesPublished research papers, found in Europe PMC
  2. 2Each fact is checked against its sourceA second AI must quote the paper word for word, and the quote is looked up
  3. 3The lesson is written, then checked againFor tone, fairness and safety
  4. 4Sources are listed in every lessonSo you can read them yourself
What is not yet done. Lessons have not been reviewed by a doctor or counsellor. When one is, the lesson will say who.
